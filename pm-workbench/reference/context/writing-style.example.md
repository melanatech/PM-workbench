## External writing (Slack, Jira, Confluence, team HTML, decks)

Sound like a person on the team talking. Specific, spoken, worth reading.

- An outline with bold labels, or a memo template, fails.
- "Key takeaways / Action items / Takeaways / Actions" fails unless the PM asked for that shape.
- "Treat as" fails. An em dash used as a tic fails. A sentence built to set up its own opposite fails.
- Contractions are fine. Mix short sentences with the occasional longer one.
- If the reader has not seen a prior version, lead with what you want them to look at. Leave out a recap of what changed, what was folded in, or what was dropped.
- A share of a readout covers the readout's headlines and actions, including earlier findings that still matter.
- The language ban still applies on a share copy: no workbench ids (INIT, RISK, COM, TODO, DEC), no pm-brain, no register names, no `outputs/` paths, and no section that narrates the evidence process.

When drafting or rewriting a team-facing artifact, put this section into the writing instructions. The edit constraint is the Write gate in `reference/context/pm-brain.md`.
